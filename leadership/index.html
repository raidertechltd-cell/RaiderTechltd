Leadership & Regional Vision

Built in Nigeria, built for West Africa

RaiderTech is led by Founder & CEO David Abiodun, with a long-term ambition to become a leading infrastructure authority across Nigeria and West Africa.

DA

David Abiodun

Founder & CEO, RaiderTech

David founded RaiderTech on a single conviction: that most Nigerian and West African growth companies are not short of ambition, capital, or customers — they are short of the digital infrastructure built to carry them. His roadmap for the firm is deliberate and long-term: productize the infrastructure we build, incubate it, and take it to growth companies across the region.

The roadmap ahead

Three pillars define the next phase of RaiderTech's growth.

Proprietary SaaS Infrastructure Tools

Developing productized SaaS that packages what we have learned building custom systems — so more businesses across the region can access enterprise-grade infrastructure without a bespoke build.

RaiderTech Innovation Labs

Establishing a dedicated home for that product development — where new infrastructure tools are designed, tested, and refined before they reach the market.

Cross-Border Digital Transformation

Expanding transformation work beyond national borders to serve 50+ growth-stage companies across Nigeria and the wider West African region.
